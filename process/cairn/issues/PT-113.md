**Gate 1 ruling (architect).** Measured at cb0a244 by driving `_test_run_shared` directly: `python3.14 scripts/cairn/run_tests.py --gate green` → `is_test_invocation=False`; `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.13 …/run_tests.py` → False; `python3 …` → True. The evasion is live on this box: `/opt/homebrew/bin/python3.14` exists (so does `python3-config`, `python3.14-config` — the reject side is real, not hypothetical).

**Seam.** One predicate, `_test_run_shared._is_python_token`, anchored on the basename:

    _PY_RE = re.compile(r"^python(3(\.\d+)?)?t?$")   # tok.rsplit("/",1)[-1]

Accept `python`, `python3`, `python3.14`, any absolute path with those basenames, and the free-threaded `python3.14t` (forward cover; *(unmeasured)* — no `t` build installed here, it costs one character). Reject `python2` (run_tests.py is py3-only; a py2 invocation produces no run), `py`, `pypy3` — brief's recommendation stands — and every dashed neighbour (`python3-config`). Anchor both ends; no `startswith`.

**Widening the guard is intended.** Guard and recorder must share one predicate or they drift (that split was PT-97 delta 1). After the fix a teammate's un-tiered `python3.14 -m unittest discover -s tests` is refused — correct, that is the hole. No lockstep change in `.claude/settings.json`: its prefilter globs `*unittest*|*run_tests*` (lines 95, 106), which is interpreter-agnostic.

**Third consumer the brief missed.** `scripts/cairn/loop_stats.py:35` imports `find_runner_invocation`/`is_full_suite_run`. Measured: `classify_bash("python3.14 scripts/cairn/run_tests.py")` returns `module_test` today, i.e. a versioned full run is invisible to the `full_suite_runs: 4` cap as well as to the guard. It must become `FULL_SUITE`. Historical `cmd` strings in the ledger reclassify with it — acceptable and desired; loop-stats is derived, not stored.
