### @team-lead — 2026-09-09

Mosko (2026-09-09, after reloading): "the chart color does not match the chart-color selection (though the color does change). Selecting Pink shows shades of brown. Selecting Amber shows shades of teal. Selecting Cyan shows shades of purple."

**Cause (from PT-118's own derivation, read off variants.json):** the rotation is Δh = variant `chart-1` hue − yellow's `chart-1` hue (98.1°), applied to families whose base hues are unrelated to yellow (counter 205°, opened 16.9°, closed 135.2°, wip 200.8°). The families keep their spread but never land on the chosen hue: Pink (Δh ≈ 252°) puts the counter family near 97° (brown); Cyan puts it near 300° (purple); even the default "yellow" renders the token chart teal. The derivation is an isometry, not an anchor — mathematically consistent, perceptually wrong.

**Ask:** the family's anchor hue equals the selected variant's `chart-1` hue. Counter family (one hue, four L/C steps — ordinal): all four take the selected hue, keeping their L/C steps; Pink → pink stacks. Flow family (three lines that must stay ≥60° apart, PT-92): ux-designer decides which series carries the selected hue (recommend `opened`, the first legend item) and the other two keep their offsets from it; the coincidence mechanism (dash + graduated markers) is hue-independent so PT-92's guards still bind. Consequence to state plainly: the default `yellow` will render yellow-anchored charts instead of today's teal/red — that is the point (the control names the colour the chart wears); the "yellow byte-identical" guard from PT-118 is replaced by "the default renders the default's hue". Gamut mapping and the contrast/distinctness sweep from PT-118 stay as-is (hue fixed, L/C nudged where a hue fails a floor — yellow-family hues near L .6 on white may need it). Acceptance: switch to Pink/Amber/Cyan, reload, both charts' rendered hues are within ±3° of the selected `chart-1` hue on their anchor series (elementFromPoint-sampled), two variants × light/dark; sweep green across all 24 × 2.

### @ux-designer — 2026-09-09

**Anchor rule (ux-designer).** Agree with the ask as framed — `opened` carries the selected `chart-1` hue directly.

**Counter family:** all four `--chart-counter-*` tokens take `h = variant.chart-1.h` exactly, keeping each token's own ratified `L`/`C`. No offset, no spread — this is already a single-hue (205°) ordinal family, so "anchor" replaces that fixed reference hue outright.

**Flow family — `opened` is the anchor, `closed`/`wip` keep their original angular offset from it (not from yellow):**
- `opened.h_new = variant.chart-1.h`
- `closed.h_new = opened.h_new + 118.3°` (today's `closed − opened` gap, `135.2 − 16.9`)
- `wip.h_new = opened.h_new + 183.9°` (today's `wip − opened` gap, `200.8 − 16.9`)
- Pairwise spacing is preserved exactly (still a pure rotation, now anchored at `opened` instead of at yellow's `chart-1`): closed↔opened 118.3°, wip↔opened 183.9°, wip↔closed 65.6° — all clear PT-92's ≥60° floor structurally, same isometry argument as PT-118, just a different anchor point. Role-collision (≥25°) is the same argument but the *specific* 322-check numbers PT-118 measured do not carry over — every family now lands at a different absolute hue than PT-118's yellow-relative rotation produced, so the gamut/contrast/ΔE sweep must re-run against this formula. The mechanism (rotate → mechanical unclamped gamut-map → measure contrast + categorical ΔE after mapping, never inferred) is unchanged.

**`opened`, not `closed`/`wip`, because it's the base reference both derivations already treat specially:** it's the bottom of the line z-order (PT-92) and the first legend item — the series a user's eye lands on first, so "I picked X and the chart is X" holds for the item most likely to be read as *the* chart's color, without requiring per-series recoloring UI.

**Consequence, stated plainly (per the ask):** `yellow`'s own charts change — `opened` moves from 16.9° to 98.111°, counter's whole family moves from 205° to 98.111°. PT-118's "yellow byte-identical" contract is retired, replaced by "the default renders the default's own hue," same as every other option. **This also retires PT-118's specific wip chroma fix as a literal value** (`0.100`/`0.097`, computed for `h 200.8`) — no variant renders at 200.8° anymore except by coincidence, so that gamut-mapped constant becomes template shape data only; the mechanical per-variant gamut-mapper (already speced) now runs for `yellow` too, not just the other 23.

**Open question for architect, not mine to rule on:** does an unqualified `:root` block ever paint before `data-cairn-chart` is applied (first paint / no-JS)? If so, app.css's literal historical hues (16.9/135.2/200.8/205) would flash briefly even under `yellow`, since those numbers are no longer the rendered `yellow` output — they're now template-only (L/C-per-step shape + the two angular offsets). Needs a mechanism answer before I write the spec's "what app.css actually contains" language.
