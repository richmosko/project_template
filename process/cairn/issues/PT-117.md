### @team-lead — 2026-09-08

Mosko (2026-09-09): "PT-110 seems to have a bug: the header is fixed at the top... but its background is transparent and scrolling content shows up behind the header. Regardless of transparency or not, the content 'top' should be the bottom of the header without it showing any higher."

**Measured on the live board @main (PT-110 merged), mist/blue light, scrollY 260:** header rect 0–89, computed `background-color oklch(0.963 0.002 197.1)` (opaque `--muted`, `opacity 1`, no background-image) — the header box itself is not transparent. What shows through is the **24 px `gap-6` strip between the header's bottom border (y 89) and the next section**: the page root is `flex flex-col gap-6 px-7 py-7`, the sticky header keeps its box in flow, and the flex gap below it is page background, so scrolling cards pass through that strip in full view (`elementFromPoint` at header-bottom +2/+12/+22 px hits the stat cards' `overflow-hidden` wrappers; first card top at −119 with its bottom edges visible at y 89–113). Perceptually the header reads as a translucent bar. Screenshot `screenshot-1788929980906-25.jpg`; zoom `screenshot-1788930029000-26.png`.

**Ask:** the content's visible top is the header's bottom edge — nothing scrolls into view above it. Candidates for the ruling: (a) the header box extends over the gap with its own opaque bottom padding (`pb-6 -mb-6`, border kept at the content edge or moved onto an inner row); (b) the page root's gap removed after the header with an explicit `mt-6` on the first content block, so the header's opaque box is contiguous with the content top; (c) a `before:`/`after:` masking element. Whichever lands, the acceptance is the same browser measurement as PT-110's AC1: at several scroll offsets, `elementFromPoint` at every y from header top to the first content block's top hits the header (or its mask), never content; light + dark, two variants; no h-scroll. Keep PT-110's guards (sticky, z-40, structural child-of-root, no spacer element — a padding/margin change is not a spacer) green or restate them deliberately. Both routes.

### @architect — 2026-09-08

**Gate 1 ruling (architect).** Measured in a real browser, not from source: worktree `vite build` → `cairn serve --port 8891` → headless Chrome over CDP, 1440×900, `/dashboard` and `/dashboard/issues`, light and dark.

**The gap is only half the defect.** At scrollY 1500 the token chart **paints over the pinned header** — 623 of 1026 hit-test points inside the header's own box return chart nodes, and the screenshot shows the title behind teal bars. Winner: `rect.lc-tooltip-rect` inside `div.lc-tooltip-context-container`, **`z-index: 50`**, against the header's `z-index: 40`. LayerChart sets that z-index itself, in the content subtree, so it outranks PT-110's header tier globally. Mosko's words — "its background is transparent and scrolling content shows up behind the header" — describe this, not only the 24 px strip. Fixing the gap alone leaves it live.

**Ruled mechanism, two parts, both measured:**
1. **`isolate` (`isolation: isolate`) on every non-header direct child of the page root.** Each becomes its own stacking context, so a library's `z-50` can no longer escape it and compete with the header. Measured: over-header hits at scrollY 1500 go **623 → 0**, every other offset stays 0. `.isolate` is already compiled into `dist/assets/index.css`.
2. **`-mb-6` on the `<header>`.** It cancels the root's flex row-gap **after the header only**, so the first content block's border-box top *is* the header's bottom edge. Measured: `next.top − header.bottom` **24 → 0** on both routes; the root's `row-gap` stays `24px` for the remaining children; no horizontal scroll appears; `/dashboard/issues` still does not scroll (PT-110 AC5).

**Candidates (a) and (c) rejected on measurement.** (a) `pb-6 -mb-6`: with the padding applied for real (inline), the header's `border-b` moves to y 113 — it then reads as the first card's top edge, which the brief explicitly rules out, and it only works at all once (1) is in place. (c) `::after` mask: measured 2 px short of the strip (leaks at y 112–113), and once the gap is zero it masks nothing. Neither is needed.

**Method warning, and a real trap for the build.** My first pass applied `pb-6 -mb-6` via `classList` and measured "no change" — because **those utilities are not in the compiled CSS** (`grep` of `dist/assets/index.css`: `.isolate` present, `.pb-6`/`.-mb-6` absent). The test passed through a no-op. So: **the acceptance must be measured on a freshly built dist, and qa's scan must assert the rule exists in the built CSS**, not merely that the class string appears in `App.svelte`. The rebuilt `dist/` is committed (PT-106).

**Acceptance — two properties, at scrolls 0 / 200 / 700 / 1500 / 2400, both routes, light and dark.**
- **A (nothing over the header):** for every y in `[header.top, header.bottom)` at 9 x positions spanning the header's width, `document.elementFromPoint(x, y)` is the header or a descendant. Baseline: `0, 0, 0, 623, 0`. With both fixes: `0` everywhere, in both themes.
- **B (content top is the header's bottom edge):** at rest, `header.nextElementSibling.getBoundingClientRect().top − header.getBoundingClientRect().bottom === 0`. Baseline 24 px, after 0 px, on `/dashboard` and `/dashboard/issues`.
- Content sliding **under** the header, clipped at its bottom border, is the intended result — verified visually in both themes.

**PT-110's guards are kept, not restated away** — verified live with both fixes applied: header `position: sticky`, `top: 0px`, `z-index: 40`, a direct child of the page-root `div.min-h-screen`, `<header>` immediately followed by `{#if !onIssueTracking}`, never `fixed`, and no spacer element. A negative margin is a margin, not a spacer.

**Guards (qa writes; red at gate 2).**
1. Source scan: `<header …>` carries `sticky top-0 z-40 … -mb-6`; each non-header direct child of the page root carries `isolate`.
2. Built-CSS scan: `dist/assets/*.css` contains both an `isolation:isolate` rule and the `-mb-6` (`margin-bottom:-1.5rem`) rule — the no-op trap above, asserted directly.
3. Dist freshness stays green (`check_dist_freshness.py`), i.e. the committed dist is the build of this source.
4. A note in the test file that properties A and B are rendered proofs owned by the lead's browser leg, with the two predicates written out verbatim so they can be re-run.

**Writers.** implementation-lead → `scripts/cairn/dashboard/src/App.svelte` + rebuilt `dist/`. qa → the scan tests above. No other file.

### @architect — 2026-09-08

**Addendum 1 to the gate-1 ruling (architect) — guard 2 restated. The objection is correct and the error is mine.** I wrote `margin-bottom:-1.5rem` into the ruling from assumption, not from the artifact: this repo's Tailwind v4 compiles every spacing utility as `calc(var(--spacing) * N)`. Verified independently at `cfa8f16`, without waiting for the implementation commit, by reading utilities already in the built CSS: `.gap-6{gap:calc(var(--spacing) * 6)}`, `.py-4{padding-block:calc(var(--spacing) * 4)}`, negatives as `margin-inline:calc(var(--spacing) * -1)`, and **zero** margin declarations containing a literal `rem` anywhere in `dist/assets/index.css`.

**Guard 2 now reads:** the built CSS (`scripts/cairn/dashboard/dist/assets/*.css`) contains
- an `isolation:isolate` declaration (already present, unchanged), and
- a rule whose selector is `.-mb-6` and whose declaration multiplies the spacing variable by −6 — match `margin-(bottom|block-end):calc\(var\(--spacing\) \* -6\)`, accepting either property name rather than pinning the one Tailwind happens to emit today.

No literal `rem` may be required by this guard, in either direction.

**The generalisable point, for this guard and future ones:** a scan of a *built* artifact must derive its expected string from the artifact, never from how you assume the toolchain compiles. The cheapest form is to read a sibling utility that is already compiled (`.gap-6` here) and assert the same shape. My no-op warning in the ruling was about exactly this failure class and I then walked into it one paragraph later.

Everything else in the ruling stands: the mechanism, properties A and B, PT-110's guards, and guards 1, 3 and 4 are unchanged. Writer: qa.
