From the architect's PT-63 review (e7299f5): with fonts landed, ~11 board
selectors at 11-12px (chips, drawer/filter labels) render in Merriweather —
the serif — which is the exact "badge/chip labels and small form labels"
risk the design spec's Accessibility section names, at sizes below what
the spec described.

**Options (Mosko's call — design decision):**
- (a) Scope the smallest components to `--font-heading` (Space Grotesk) —
  cheapest (~11 selectors); face-is-by-role arguably makes labels sans
  anyway. Architect-recommended; aligns with the spec's own
  "keep the smallest text out of the serif" recommendation.
- (b) Bump 11-12px to 14px per the spec's literal alternative — changes
  board density materially.
- (c) Accept and record on localhost-dev-tool grounds.

**Acceptance criteria** (assuming (a); revise if (b)/(c) chosen)
- [ ] The sub-13px selector set renders in a sans face (or the chosen
      alternative), enumerated mechanically (every selector under 13px)
- [ ] Guard pinning the face-floor rule so future small text can't
      silently go serif
- [ ] Spec's Accessibility section updated to record the resolution
