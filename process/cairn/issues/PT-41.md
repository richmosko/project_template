## Problem

Mosko (2026-08-23): the sole major is named `PT-V1` (file + id) while every milestone under it tags 0.x — the name reads as "version 1" but the line is pre-1.0. Candidates raised: `PT-V0.x`, `PT-V1.x` — or keep `PT-V1` with its meaning ("the line that culminates in v1.0.0 GA") made explicit. Naming semantics ripple into the versioning scheme (WORKFLOW.md), the ga-milestone convention, check_repo's major id shape (PT-28's lint), and a possible named migration.
