## Problem

PT-85 introduced three dedicated categorical tokens for the throughput chart —
`--chart-flow-opened`, `--chart-flow-closed`, `--chart-flow-wip` — with interim values taken
from the dataviz skill's validated categorical palette, chosen by implementation-lead under two
constraints only: mutually distinguishable in every theme variant, and not coinciding with a
`--chart-role-*` hue used by the token/cost chart on the same dashboard page. No design review.

## Ask

ux-designer ratifies or replaces the three values against the design system (docs/DESIGN,
tokens.css preset). Names and the per-variant distinguishability guard are the contract; values
may change. If replaced, update app.css and every variants.css entry, rebuild dist, and re-run the
guard.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A decision recorded on this issue: ratified as-is, or replaced with the new values and why.
2. If replaced: the guard test green in every variant, light and dark; dist rebuilt and committed.
3. Browser-visibility pass on the throughput chart in at least two variants (light + dark).

### @ux-designer — 2026-09-08

**Decision (ux-designer): 2 of 3 values RATIFIED, 1 REPLACED.**

**Ratified as-is:** `--chart-flow-opened` (both modes), `--chart-flow-wip` (both modes), `--chart-flow-closed` dark (`oklch(0.65 0.15 135.2)`, 5.69:1 vs the dark card). The architect's own hue placements (16.9/135.2/200.8°) and implementation-lead's independently-tuned L/C already clear every hard floor (role-collision ≥25°, pairwise ≥60°, lightness/chroma band, normal-vision, contrast) with real margin — re-deriving them would only add risk for no gain.

**Replaced:** `--chart-flow-closed` light mode only, `oklch(0.68 0.18 135.2)` → `oklch(0.62 0.18 135.2)` (C, H unchanged — both hue-only guards untouched). PT-85 accepted a 2.72:1-vs-white contrast WARN on the strength of the "always carries a legend/tooltip" mitigation — calibrated for a filled bar's area, not a dashed line's stroke. Under this ticket's own coincidence fix (below), `closed` is a dashed line drawn *on top of* `opened`'s solid line, so at any shared x the reader resolves `closed` from its dash gaps alone — the low-area, high-precision case the bar-shaped mitigation doesn't cover. Measured (own OKLCH→sRGB + WCAG luminance reimplementation — dataviz skill's `validate_palette.js` unreachable this session, same standing caveat the doc already carries): old `#63af2a` vs `#ffffff` = 2.72:1; new `#509c06` vs `#ffffff` = 3.44:1 in both the float and quantized-hex models (PT-69 two-model check) — clears WCAG 1.4.11's 3:1 non-text-contrast floor outright, no WARN. Flagged trade: a deutan re-simulation (Machado/Oliveira/Fairchild 2009) shows `opened`/`closed` separation narrows slightly at the darker L, while `closed`/`wip` widens by more; light mode carried no CVD WARN before (unlike dark's already-accepted opened/closed ΔE 7.1), so this isn't a new risk band, but re-run through the actual skill script before final lock. Dark `closed` is unchanged (no bar→line contrast risk there, already 5.69:1).

**Line treatment (binding on implementation-lead), answering the gate-1 coincidence requirement:**

- **Stroke:** `opened` solid 2px, bottom z-order. `closed` dashed 2px (dasharray `6,4`), drawn on top of `opened`. `wip` dotted 2.5px (dasharray `2,4`), drawn on top of both. Dash-on-solid means at an exact coincidence the top series' gaps expose the series beneath — both remain independently identifiable along any coincident run ≥ one dash cycle (~10px), which is what the gate-1 ruling requires the mechanism to survive. The three distinct periodicities (continuous / 6-4 / 2-4) also mean no x-window is ever uniformly covered by only the topmost color even where all three coincide.
- **WIP-on-axis remedy:** the dotted stroke is deliberately non-continuous and paired with its own point markers (below) — a real axis rule has neither gaps nor discrete points, so `wip` at y=0 never visually fuses with it. No change requested to `BarChart`'s own axis styling.
- **Markers:** filled circle at every data vertex, per series, same z-order as the strokes, **graduated radius**: `opened` r=5 (bottom), `closed` r=3.5 (middle), `wip` r=2 with a 1px `--card`-colored outline ring (top). At an exact coincidence of two or three series, the larger marker's rim stays visible around the smaller one nested inside it — this is what answers the gate-1 "hover the coincident point (2026-09-07, opened=closed=3) and confirm both are still individually identifiable" check at the mark level; the tooltip (existing, `BarChart`-owned) is the other half, and should keep listing all three by name+color+stroke-style regardless of dash phase at the hovered pixel.
- **Legend:** fixed order **Opened, Closed, WIP** (token declaration order = semantic reading order: inflow, outflow, current-state snapshot). Each swatch previews stroke style (solid/dashed/dotted) + marker, not just the color chip, so the legend itself teaches the non-color channel a reader needs at a coincident point.
- **Caption/tooltip:** name all three series, same fixed order, at every x.

**Files touched (this commit):** `dashboard/src/app.css` (light `--chart-flow-closed` value + comment), `dashboard/src/variants.css` (23 light-mode `--chart-flow-closed` occurrences, same replacement, dark untouched), `docs/DESIGN/design-system-spec.md` (Chart-local ramp section: table + PT-92 resolution + line-treatment summary). Did not touch `IssueFlowChart.svelte` or `dashboard/dist` — implementation-lead's per the gate-1 ruling.
