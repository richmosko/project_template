## Comments

### @team-lead — 2026-09-08

Mosko (2026-09-09): "chart-color in the top right settings no longer seems to take effect."

**Measured on the live board @main:** the preference mechanism itself works — switching Chart Color from `yellow` to `lime` sets `data-cairn-chart="lime"` on the root and `--chart-1` goes from `oklch(90.5% .182 98.111)` to `oklch(89.7% .196 126.665)`. Nothing consumes those tokens any more: the throughput chart draws from fixed `--chart-flow-opened/closed/wip` (PT-85 retired the ramp for that chart; PT-92 ratified the three hues), and the token chart draws from `--chart-counter-input/cache-write/cache-read/output` and `--chart-role-1..7` (PT-88/PT-101/PT-104), all defined once in app.css, not per chart variant — 56 bars rendered, every fill `oklch(0.65 0.0777 205)` regardless of the setting. So the setting is a no-op; the 24 chart variants in variants.css style tokens no chart reads.

**Ask (ux-designer + architect):** decide what "Chart Color" means now that charts are semantic (opened/closed/wip; input/cache/output; roles), and make the setting true to it. Candidates: (a) derive the chart-local tokens from the selected ramp per variant in `design/variants.json` → `gen_variants.py` (hue family follows the choice, the semantic distinctness guards and contrast floors from PT-92/PT-83 must still pass in every variant × mode); (b) retire the Chart Color control and its 24 variants as dead, with the removal recorded in the design spec; (c) keep the control but scope it to the token chart's role palette only, relabelled. Acceptance: whichever lands is browser-verified — change the setting, reload, and the affected chart's rendered fills/strokes change (elementFromPoint-sampled), or the control is gone; the palette guards stay green; both variants × light/dark. Not a PT-110 regression — measured against the PT-85 date.
