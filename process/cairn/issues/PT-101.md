Found on PT-88's visual leg (PT-88.md @ cda6770) and framed by the architect at the verdict: in chronological mode every issue is a column (~13 px wide at 1746 px viewport) on a shared scale whose top is ~800 M tokens, so two issues render at 0.489 px and 0.078 px — present in the DOM, invisible to a human, indistinguishable from an issue with no recorded usage.
