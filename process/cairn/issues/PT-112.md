### @architect — 2026-09-08

**Gate 1 ruling (architect).** Measured live at `05557de`, not from the brief. I ran a narrowed `python3 -m unittest discover -s tests -p "test_message_cap_hook.py" … | tail -3` from this worktree and read the record the hook wrote: `{"ts": "2026-09-09T04:45:31.000Z", "who": "architect", "session": "817d7be4-be06-4fca-b274-259509f1934d", "runner": "unittest"}`. My own Bash environment reports `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID=817d7be4-be06-4fca-b274-259509f1934d`. **The hook's stdin `session_id` and the runner's own `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID` are the same string** — a deterministic key already available on both sides, needing no new channel. Corroboration: for 5 of 5 sampled ledger records with a non-null `session`, `<session>.jsonl` exists in the transcripts dir and its role equals the record's `who`.

**Ruling on (1): the key is the session id. No run id, and nothing is read back from stdout.** `run_tests.py::_self_record` writes `"session": None` today (~L392); it must write `os.environ.get("CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID") or None`. The hook matches its payload's `session_id` against that field. The brief's run-id-in-the-summary-line is rejected: the summary is the channel PT-97 gate-4 delta 2 deliberately abandoned, and PT-111 measured **49 of 165** real full-run steps whose stdout was redirected or suppressed (`nohup`, `> file`, `--json`), so an id printed there is unreadable exactly when it matters. `cmd` + `ts` proximity is also rejected: two teammates can type the identical command, and PT-111 measured what windows do — a ±window promoted 3 of 5 real guard denials by pairing them with an unrelated record.

**Ruling on (2): scan a bounded tail, match, and never adopt a stranger.** Replace `_patch_last_who` (~L82) with a scan of the **last 200 lines** for the **newest** record satisfying **all** of: `who is null`, `session` non-null, `session == payload["session_id"]`. Patch that line only.
- No match and the command's runner is `run_tests` → **write nothing** (the run recorded itself; unattributed beats misattributed).
- No match and the runner is `unittest` → scrape-and-append exactly as today; `session` comes from the payload, so the appended record is self-attributing.
- A record whose `session` is null is **never** adopted — that is the stale-line defect, and it is also what every pre-change record looks like. Those stay `who: null` permanently, by design.

**Ruling on (3): PT-107's in-place-patch fixture must change, deliberately.** `test_the_post_hook_patches_who_in_place_rather_than_appending_a_second_record` (test_worktree_metrics_path_resolution.py) writes a null-`who` record with no `session`; under this ruling that record is no longer patchable. qa updates the fixture to carry a `session` equal to the payload's, and adds the negative control alongside it. The worktree redirect and `CAIRN_TEST_RUNS_FILE` path resolution are untouched.

**In scope: the patch is a read-modify-write of the whole file.** `_patch_last_who` does `read_text() → splitlines() → write_text()`; a record appended by another worktree's runner between the read and the write is silently lost. That is the same concurrency this issue exists to fix, so hold `fcntl.flock(LOCK_EX)` across the read-modify-write in the hook, and never raise if locking is unavailable (a hook must not break the call it is attached to). Runner appends stay plain `"a"`-mode writes.

**Ruling on (4):** `loop_stats._match_ledger` is bookkeeping only since PT-111 delta 1 — no change, and no test in this feature may depend on it.

**Guards (qa writes; red at gate 2).**
1. Two interleaved records, both `who: null`, sessions `S1` and `S2`, in that order; hook payload for `S1` patches the **first** record and leaves the second null; then the payload for `S2` patches the second. Today's last-line rule fails the first assertion.
2. Stale null line: a null-`who`, null-`session` record at the tail is never patched by any payload.
3. Cross-attribution control: a payload whose `session_id` matches **no** record patches nothing and appends nothing for a `run_tests` command.
4. `unittest` runner with no matching record still appends a scraped record carrying the payload's `session` and `who`.
5. Runner: `_self_record` writes `session` from `CLAUDE_CODE_SESSION_ID`, and `None` when the variable is absent — both directions, env set and unset.
6. Concurrency: a subprocess appends a line while the hook holds the file for its patch; the appended line survives and the patched line is correct. Without the lock the appended line is lost.
7. No-regression: PT-107 (updated fixture as above) and PT-113 hook tests green.

**Out of scope, observed while measuring (follow-up candidate, do not fix here).** A *failing* narrowed unittest run I made minutes earlier (exit 1, summary visible in the piped stdout) produced **no ledger record at all** — one construction, 2026-09-09T04:4xZ, nothing appended to either checkout's ledger. Either the PostToolUse hook does not fire on a non-zero exit or the error payload carries stdout elsewhere; worth its own issue, since it means red runs are invisible to loop-stats.
